1. Scan files from this device

Choose a whole folder such as Downloads, Documents, Pictures or DCIM. Its files and subfolders are included. Only your chosen folders are scanned.

Select the folder itself, then tap Use this folder or Select. For photos, choose DCIM, Pictures or an album's storage folder; photos and videos in its subfolders are included. Virtual albums inside the Gallery app are not device folders. If you only see individual files, open this page in an updated Chrome browser. Files over 128 MB are skipped to protect browser memory.

By default, file contents stay on your device. File names, sizes and full-file fingerprints are sent to your account for exact matching. Scanning never deletes or renames your originals.
